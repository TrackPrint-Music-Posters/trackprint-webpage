A3Styles

Eight styles.
One album.

Every style lays out the same album its own way, each with its own typefaces. Flip through them and pick the one that fits your wall.

  1. 01

    Classic

    The cover, the title, five colours from the cover and the tracklist in two columns.

  2. 02

    Tracklist

    The songs run together above the cover, the title in capitals below it.

  3. 03

    Vinyl

    The songs along one spiral groove, with the cover as the record’s label.

  4. 04

    Aura

    A colour field mixed from the cover, with the running time as a playback bar.

  5. 05

    Runtime

    Every track as a bar as long as it plays, in one gradient of the cover’s colours.

  6. 06

    Editorial

    A magazine cover on paper, the year in Roman numerals and the title in italics.

  7. 07

    Swiss

    Flat shapes in the cover’s strongest colour and the title turned up the edge.

  8. 08

    Liner Notes

    A cassette J-card in monospace, the cover in halftone and a barcode of the songs.

Or a size of your own. Export as JPEG, PNG or PDF.

B1Now Playing

Every style
plays along.

See what the Music app is playing in a player drawn after each of the eight styles. Open the album and tap a song to play it, or go full screen.

  1. 01

    Classic

    The Poster. The poster in its crop marks, its five colours filling in turn as the song plays.

  2. 02

    Tracklist

    Wall of Words. The album’s songs run together, the one playing lit up as far as it has played.

  3. 03

    Vinyl

    The Groove. The record turns at 33⅓, the album’s songs along its groove.

  4. 04

    Aura

    Glow. The cover under a warm halo, glass buttons and what plays next.

  5. 05

    Runtime

    Instrument. The time played, huge, over the song’s ten-second segments on graph paper.

  6. 06

    Editorial

    Magazine. The artist as the masthead and an opening paragraph on what plays.

  7. 07

    Swiss

    Bauhaus. The huge circle, the track’s number giant and the title up the edge.

  8. 08

    Liner Notes

    Open Case. A cassette case lying open, its reels winding as the album plays.

B2Lock Screen

Right on the
Lock Screen.

On iPhone the song playing stays on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island, in the player’s style, with play, pause and skip.

B3Mini Player

Over
every app.

On the Mac the mini player floats the song playing over your work, in four sizes, from a thin strip to a square with the album’s tracks.

PagesFileEditViewSat 9:41

Window ▸ Show Mini Player, or ⇧⌘M. Right-click it to change its size and style, keep it on top or show it on every desktop.

B4Notch

Round
the notch.

The notch player draws the song’s progress round your MacBook’s notch. Rest the pointer on it and it opens into the player. On a screen without a notch it’s a small pill in the menu bar.
