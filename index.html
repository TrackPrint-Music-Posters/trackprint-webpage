A3Styles

Eight styles.
One album.

Every style lays out the same album its own way, each with its own typefaces. Flip through them and pick the one that fits your wall.

  1. 01

    Classic

    The cover, the title, five colours from the cover and the tracklist in two columns.

  2. 02

    Tracklist

    The songs run together above the cover, the title in capitals below it.

  3. 03

    Vinyl

    The songs along one spiral groove, with the cover as the record’s label.

  4. 04

    Aura

    A colour field mixed from the cover, with the running time as a playback bar.

  5. 05

    Runtime

    Every track as a bar as long as it plays, in one gradient of the cover’s colours.

  6. 06

    Editorial

    A magazine cover on paper, the year in Roman numerals and the title in italics.

  7. 07

    Swiss

    Flat shapes in the cover’s strongest colour and the title turned up the edge.

  8. 08

    Liner Notes

    A cassette J-card in monospace, the cover in halftone and a barcode of the songs.
