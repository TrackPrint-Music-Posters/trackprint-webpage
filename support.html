Support

Answers to the questions people ask most, and a form to report a bug or ask for a feature. You can also write to mateoas@icloud.com.

Questions

Where do the albums come from?

From MusicBrainz, the open music encyclopedia, with covers from the Cover Art Archive. Searching needs no account and no permission.

TrackPrint can’t find an album, or says MusicBrainz is busy.

Try a shorter search with the album’s title and the artist. MusicBrainz is a free, community-run service and sometimes answers slowly; TrackPrint keeps trying for a few seconds, and trying again a moment later usually works.

How big can I print a poster?

Print is 15 × 20 cm and Poster 20 × 30 cm, both at 300 ppi, the resolution print shops ask for. Post and Story are sized for screens. Export as PDF or PNG for the sharpest print, and check a test print first: colours on paper never quite match a screen.

How do my posters get to my other devices?

Through your own iCloud account. Sign in with the same Apple Account on each device and leave iCloud Sync on in TrackPrint’s Settings. Turned off, your posters stay on the device you made them on. We never see your posters.

Can I share or sell my posters?

A poster for your own wall or as a gift is fine, and so is having it printed on paper at a print shop. Posting a poster with a real album cover online, or selling it, needs the permission of the rights holders: the label, the artist and the photographer. The Post and Story sizes don’t give that permission. See section 4 of the terms of use.

Why does TrackPrint ask me to agree to its terms?

Because a poster carries other people’s work. The terms of use say what’s fine, like a poster for your wall or a gift, and what needs permission. You agree once, and can read them again in Settings ▸ About.

What does TrackPrint collect about me?

Nothing for analytics, ads or tracking, and there is no account. Searches go straight to MusicBrainz, and your posters stay on your devices and in your iCloud. The privacy policy has the details.

For rights holders

TrackPrint is a free app that lays out an album’s cover, title and tracklist as a poster for the user’s own wall. It hosts nothing: covers come from the Cover Art Archive, posters stay on the user’s devices and in their private iCloud, and we never see them.

What we can do

  • Stop the app finding an album or artist, from the next app update.
  • Remove any image of your work from this website or our App Store pages within days.
  • Point you to the source: the Cover Art Archive takes requests at info@archive.org.

What to send us

  • Who you are, and the rights you hold or represent.
  • The album, artist or image, and where you saw it.
  • What you’d like us to do.
  • A statement that your notice is accurate and made in good faith.

Write to mateoas@icloud.com. We reply within 7 days.

Send Feedback

Found a bug or missing something? Tell us here; no account needed. The app has the same form in Settings ▸ Send Feedback.

Kind

Your report goes to TrackPrint’s private issue tracker. Leave out anything personal you don’t want us to read; to hear back, write to us by email instead.
